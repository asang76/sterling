# Content guidelines

- UK English ("enquiry", "licence", "organisation"). Sentence case. Short, specific, plain.
- Every claim needs evidence the owner can show. If not, write `[CONFIRM ...]` or leave it out.
- Banned until proven: "CQC registered", "fully compliant", "fully vetted", "all drivers vetted", "DBS checked" (for everyone), "24/7", "same day", "nationwide", "leading", "number one", any statistic, review, client name or award.
- Care and support copy: checks and suitability depend on the role. Do not imply every worker has every check.
- Vacancies: only real, current roles in `src/content/jobs.ts`. Remove or close a role when filled.
- Editing page copy: service, employer, candidate, FAQ and complaints pages come from `src/content/pages.json` (types in `src/content/types.ts`). Legal pages, About, Home, Contact and Jobs are in their own `page.tsx`.
- Each page needs a unique title (under about 60 characters), a unique description (under about 160), one H1, and a clear next step.
- Articles (future): only if someone can keep them accurate and reviewed. No bulk generated posts.
